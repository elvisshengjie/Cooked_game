Controls:

Movement: WASD
Pause: T
Dash: Shift
Pick up/Drop ingredients/ Activate Repellent: E
Cook: Q
QTE gauge: R
Clean(stove): C
Repellent: E

- Score 30 points before Timer runs out
- Gather ingredients for any recipe
- Next ingredient randomly spawns
- unwanted ingredient can be thrown away 
- Chase mice away using the mouse repellent
